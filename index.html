What we don't do

Florida law requires a licensed pro for these, even small ones. We'll point you to one.

  • Electrical: light fixtures, ceiling fans, outlets, switches
  • Plumbing: faucets, toilets, water heaters
  • HVAC, roofing and structural work
  • Any job of $2,500 or more, or one that needs a permit
