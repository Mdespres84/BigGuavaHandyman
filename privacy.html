Privacy

Last updated October 5, 2026.

What we collect

When you send an estimate request, we receive what you enter: your name, phone, email, ZIP code, optional street address, the jobs you picked and any notes. Requests are delivered to our inbox by Web3Forms, a form delivery service.

How we use it

Only to reply about your job, schedule it and keep a record of the work. We don't sell or share your information, and we don't send marketing without asking.

Your list on this device

The jobs you add are saved in your browser's local storage so your list survives a page refresh. It never leaves your device unless you send it. Clear it any time by removing the jobs or clearing your browser data.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies, loads nothing from other websites, and uses no advertising or tracking scripts.

Contact

To see or delete what we have about you, email hello@bigguavahandyman.com.
