TV Mounting in Tampa

We mount your TV on studs, level it, and leave the cords tidy. Bring your own mount or we'll supply one. Most single-TV jobs take about an hour.

What's included

  • Stud location and a level, secure mount
  • Connecting the devices you already have
  • Cleanup and a quick walkthrough

Good to know

  • We don't move outlets or run power inside the wall. That's electrical work, and Florida requires a licensed electrician for it.
  • Over-fireplace mounts depend on the brick or stone. Send a photo and we'll confirm.

Questions about tv mounting

Do I need to buy the mount?

No. Choose "Big Guava supplies the mount" and we'll send you a few options, billed at our cost plus 15%. Or choose "I'll supply the mount" and have yours on hand.

Can you hide the wires?

We install a paintable cord cover on the wall surface. Running power inside the wall needs a licensed electrician.
